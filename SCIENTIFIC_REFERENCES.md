## Required reporting

Report the software version, operating system, Python version, input data range,
wavelength model, geometry, background order, profile model, weighting scheme,
refined and fixed parameters, constraints, convergence settings, excluded
regions, uncertainty treatment, and all agreement indices. Preserve the
observed, calculated, and difference profiles in the research record.

The provided NaCl/CsCl datasets are deterministic software-test fixtures. They
must not be described as certified diffraction standards or experimental data.
